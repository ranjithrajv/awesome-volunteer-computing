# Legacy Projects

These projects have historical significance in volunteer computing but are no longer actively maintained or have been discontinued. They are preserved here for reference.

> **Note:** These entries are excluded from the main list per awesome-list guidelines, which recommend against featuring unmaintained or archived projects.

---

- 👽 **[SETI@home](https://setiathome.berkeley.edu/)** ([Wikidata](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q330835)) - Based at UC Berkeley, a scientific experiment to Search for Extraterrestrial Intelligence (SETI). Went into hibernation in 2020.
- 🧪 **[XtremWeb](http://www.xtremweb.net/)** - Research platform for global distributed computing used by academic institutions. No longer actively maintained.
- 📲 **[DreamLab](https://www.vodafone.com/vodafone-foundation/focus-areas/dreamlab-app)** - Vodafone Foundation's mobile app that powered medical and nutritional research while your phone charges.
- 🔧 **[Volunteer Grid Toolkit (VGT)](https://sourceforge.net/projects/vgt/)** - A toolkit for experimenting with volunteer grid computing concepts.
